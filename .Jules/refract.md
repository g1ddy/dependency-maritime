## 2024-05-30 - [Hardcoded Types/Strings in Component Props/Logic] **Observation:** Identified hardcoded string literals used for properties that have defined enums or specific union types in external libraries (e.g., `@xyflow/react`'s `Position` enum). This leads to reliance on `as any` casting to appease the compiler and defeats the purpose of TypeScript. **Strategy:** Always prefer importing and utilizing defined enums or strict literal types provided by libraries over hardcoding primitive strings and casting. This enforces compile-time type safety and improves maintainability.
