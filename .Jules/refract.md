## 2024-11-20 - [Legacy HTMLAttributes Usage] **Observation:** Usage of `React.HTMLAttributes<HTMLElement>` drops the `ref` prop or leads to incomplete typing when combined with modern generic props spreading in React 19 functional components. **Strategy:** Recommend using `React.ComponentProps<"element">` over `React.HTMLAttributes<HTMLElement>` to ensure strict typing and seamless `ref` forwarding.
